    Shelf levels

    The minimum packs to keep for each medicine. This is the only setting the app really needs.

    You do not have to type thousands of numbers. The app can work out a sensible shelf level for every medicine from its own sales history - roughly how many it sells per day, multiplied by how long your distributor takes to deliver, plus a safety margin. Accept those in one click, then hand-adjust only your fast movers.
